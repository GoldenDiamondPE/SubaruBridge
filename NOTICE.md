# Source provenance

This public source subset contains original SubaruBridge Java, Rust, C, Python,
HTML, CSS and SVG work. The connection-check UI and event engine were originally
developed as owner-authored components in a private research adapter; the vendor
adapter and runtime are not included. No STARLINK application code or artwork is
licensed or redistributed here.

SubaruBridge grew out of research in the GPLv3
[MirrorLink-Pi project](https://github.com/rsyrnicki/mirror-link-pi).
Its earlier Pi/scrcpy paths remain in the separate development tree. This public
subset follows the project's GPLv3 license. Preserve notices and source license
obligations when sharing modified builds.

Android SDK/NDK, Android Gradle Plugin, Gradle and Rust are external build tools
downloaded from their maintainers and subject to their respective licenses.
They are not vendored into this repository. The ownership model uses pinned
serde and serde_json dependencies and their transitive crates. Their license
notices are listed in [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).
Icons and dashboard assets here are original;
they are not Apple or Subaru interface assets.

Product names identify interoperability targets and do not indicate endorsement.
